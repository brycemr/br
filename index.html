Current Role

I am a Machine Learning Engineer at Blissway, a Y Combinator-backed startup modernizing highway tolling and roadway safety with roadside AI devices and cloud analytics. As a founding member of the ML team, I have built our computer vision systems from the ground up — including a vision-based vehicle triggering system, axle counting, lane classification, and custom license plate recognition models — and redesigned our ML inference pipeline into optimized microservices that significantly reduced inference costs. These systems now process more than 6 million events — over 15 million images — every day.
