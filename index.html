Current Role

I am a Machine Learning Engineer at Blissway, a Y Combinator-backed startup modernizing highway tolling and roadway safety with roadside AI devices and cloud analytics. As a founding member of the ML team, I have built our computer vision systems from the ground up — vision-based vehicle triggering, axle counting, lane geometry and registration, vehicle re-identification, and custom license plate recognition models — and re-architected our inference pipeline into per-service microservices that cut serving cost while handling millions of roadside events every day. The models run both in the cloud and on NPUs mounted to poles on live interstates, so the constraints are physical and you find out quickly when you are wrong.

What I Work On

Perception for roadway infrastructure — replacing gantries, in-road sensor loops and windshield transponders with cameras and models. Some of it is deep learning; a lot of it is classical geometry, matching and evaluation infrastructure, because that is what the problem calls for.

Vehicle Triggering

Detecting and tracking vehicles on an edge NPU to decide when one has been fully captured — the vision replacement for an in-road induction loop.

Lane Geometry & Registration

Projective geometry that keeps each camera's stored lane model aligned as poles shake and cameras drift, so a vehicle's lane is never silently mislabelled downstream.

Axle Counting

Fusing per-frame wheel detections across several cameras into a single virtual chassis per vehicle, with a confidence that routes the hard cases to human review.

Vehicle Re-Identification

Fine-tuned vision transformer embeddings that match the same vehicle across cameras and viewpoints, plus make and model verification as a cross-check on plate reads.

License Plate Recognition

A multi-head reader — plate segmentation, rectification, then text, state and design — trained in house and measured against the commercial incumbent.

Camera Placement Simulation

An analytic optics and geometry simulator that picks camera aim, zoom and illumination for a site, so siting questions get answered without a field trip.
